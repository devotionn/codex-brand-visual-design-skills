# Visual quality gates

Use these gates for concept review and final critique. Avoid fake numeric scores for taste.

## Brand and communication

- Is the primary message clear before decorative detail?
- Does the direction feel appropriate to the category, audience, price position, and intended emotion?
- Is there a memorable organizing idea rather than a collection of fashionable effects?

## Hierarchy and composition

- Can a viewer identify first, second, and third reading priorities?
- Are alignment, balance, scale, rhythm, whitespace, crop, and focal point deliberate?
- Does the composition survive the actual aspect ratio, trim, or viewing distance?

## Typography

- Are typefaces chosen for a reason beyond trend?
- Are hierarchy, line length, leading, tracking, punctuation, bilingual alignment, and number treatment coherent?
- Are licenses and output requirements known for fonts used in deliverables?

## Color and imagery

- Are colors assigned roles rather than scattered decoratively?
- Is contrast adequate for the medium and viewing condition?
- Do imagery, lighting, material, lens language, retouching, and grain belong to one visual world?

## System consistency

- Do repeated modules share rules?
- Can the identity extend across more than one mockup or hero page?
- Are exceptions intentional and documented?

## Production

- Are resolution, bleed, safe areas, crop behavior, color mode, export profile, and finishing constraints appropriate?
- Are claims of readiness limited to checks actually performed?
