# Brief intake

Extract or infer the smallest useful design brief before substantial visual work.

## Core brief

- business / brand / product
- objective: what this design must accomplish
- audience: who must notice, understand, trust, desire, or act
- message: one primary message and supporting messages
- deliverables: logo, brochure, KV, image set, poster, etc.
- medium: print, screen, signage, packaging, social, presentation, web asset
- dimensions / page count / aspect ratios when known
- required content and assets
- existing brand constraints
- references and what the user likes or dislikes about them
- production constraints: printer, stock, finishing, deadline, budget, language

## Missing information

Do not ask for every field mechanically. Ask only when the missing answer could materially change the design direction, dimensions, cost, or correctness. Otherwise proceed with a stated working assumption.

## Source truth

Mark missing copy, photography, product data, certifications, company claims, and customer logos as placeholders or unresolved inputs. Never fabricate them to make the layout look complete.
