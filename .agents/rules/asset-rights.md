# Asset and reference handling

Use user-supplied and licensed assets according to their stated purpose. Keep provenance visible when it matters to delivery.

## References

Use reference work to analyze principles such as layout, contrast, proportion, typography, material, lighting, art direction, or production technique. Do not trace another company's logo, reproduce distinctive branded artwork, or present a near-copy as original work.

When the user asks for a direction inspired by a named brand or artist, translate the request into higher-level visual traits and build a new composition and identity from those traits.

## Fonts, stock, and generated imagery

- Do not assume commercial font or stock-image licenses exist.
- Mark licensing or release status as unknown when not provided.
- Treat AI-generated images as generated source material; they still need selection, factual checks, retouching, composition, and output preparation.
- Do not fabricate a model release, property release, trademark clearance, or legal clearance.

## Logo uniqueness

A visual similarity check can flag obvious collisions, but it is not a trademark search or legal clearance. State that limitation when uniqueness risk matters.
